Planning blueprint · no live effects

Missed-call responder

A legitimate caller reaches the business when nobody can answer, and the reason for the call may be lost.

01 · Starting signal

Trigger

An inbound call ends unanswered during an explicitly configured coverage window.

02 · Permitted response

Allowed actions

  1. Classify the unanswered-call event against approved business hours and routing rules.
  2. Prepare one approved acknowledgement for an eligible, consented contact route.
  3. Create a bounded follow-up item with the original event identity and no invented urgency.

03 · Data + permission

Consent check

A caller number and call metadata are personal data. Purpose, channel permission, retention, suppression, and any provider role must be explicit before an outbound effect.

04 · Human ownership

Handoff

If consent, urgency, identity, routing, or the approved response is uncertain, prepare a human-review item and make no outbound contact.

05 · Hard stop

Never allowed

Never claim an emergency response, diagnose intent, record or transcribe a call without a separate lawful basis, promise availability, or contact a person through an unapproved channel.

06 · Kill switch

Stop before the next effect.

A cell-level pause blocks every new outbound effect while preserving the minimum event evidence needed for review and duplicate suppression.

This planning cell is open for inspection. Nothing is connected or running.

This planner performs no network request, stores no business or customer data, sends no message, changes no calendar, touches no invoice, and connects to no outside system. It produces an inspectable plan so unsafe assumptions are visible before implementation is considered.

Six recurring pressure points

Start where the business loses attention.

The useful question is not “What can automation do?” It is “Which exact event deserves a bounded response, with whose permission, under whose control?”

Calls and questions

Missed calls · after hours

Acknowledge only eligible events and answer only from approved facts. Urgency, uncertainty, sensitive topics, or missing consent stop the cell and route to a person.

Appointments and trust

Booking · reviews

Keep availability provisional until the exact provider receipt exists. Ask neutrally for honest feedback without screening sentiment or creating pressure.

Promises and payment

Follow-up · getting paid

Preserve purpose, permission, exact record identity, disputes, suppression, and a clear human owner. No cell creates a charge, fee, threat, or new marketing purpose.

Architecture before activity

A serious system begins with the stop button.

The Hive planner gives you the questions a responsible build must answer. A production system would require a separately scoped architecture, reviewed providers, tested permissions, monitoring, recovery, and explicit build authority.
