Where the maker fits

Make the page, save the direction, then control the release.

The four-stage maker handles account-free page creation and review. The account and project begin only when you save, then handle the address, access, accepted releases, publication, management, export, and exit.

Before the account

Business, Look, Review, Preview

Make a working private page without an account. The details and versions stay in the open tab unless you decide to keep them.

When you save

Account, project, address, and access

Carry the reviewed preview into a project, choose a licensed Site Sourcery address or customer-owned domain path, and decide whether the release will be public or access-controlled.

After saving

Accept, publish, manage, export, and exit

Save an accepted release, publish or pause it, roll back, manage its address and access, export the project, detach a customer-owned domain, cancel, or delete.

Current private build Account, project, release, publish, and exit effects remain local to this device in a non-transactional rehearsal. Multi-tab writing is unsupported and not prevented, so use one owner tab; this is not durable or authoritative hosted persistence. There is no hosted concurrency, real charge, subscription, payment receipt, email, DNS change, or outside publication here.

Before you start

Bring enough detail to make the page useful.

The form requires a name, one factual sentence, at least one supporting detail, and one way for a visitor to act.

Required foundation

Name + one clear sentence

State the business name and what it does in plain language. Add About text, an offering, a location, or hours so the page has useful substance.

Visitor next step

Phone, email, or outside website

Add at least one. If one action should stand out, select that same phone, email, or website in the emphasis field.

  1. 01

    Business

    Write what customers should know.

    Use the exact fields in the maker: business name, what the business does, About, offerings, location or service area, hours, phone, email, outside website, and emphasized contact action.

    Put one offering on each line, up to six. Blank details stay out. Abracadabra supplies ordinary structural headings, while the business statements and contact details come from the information you enter.

  2. 02

    Look

    Choose how the same details should feel.

    Clear uses a structured split composition. Warm uses a centered editorial composition. Arcane uses a deeper, more dramatic composition.

    Changing the look does not rewrite the business details.

  3. 03

    Review

    Read the page details before making it.

    The review shows the normalized details and the chosen contact emphasis. Paragraph breaks in About remain visible.

    If any detail changes after you reach Review, Abracadabra updates the review and asks you to confirm it again before it makes a version.

  4. 04

    Preview

    Revise without throwing earlier work away.

    The embedded frame gives you a safe first look. Open working preview lets you test the page in its own tab. Edit the business details or change the look to make another version.

    Previous version and Project versions restore earlier accepted releases. Download this HTML saves a portable copy of the reviewed page.

Quick recovery

If something interrupts the flow.

Every message should lead to a concrete next move.

Abracadabra did not finish opening.

Reload the page. The workroom remains locked when a required local script does not load.

A field is highlighted.

Read the named message at the top of the workroom and correct that field. Contact emphasis also requires the matching contact detail.

The review changed.

A business detail changed after the prior review. Read the updated version and confirm it again.

A link does not work inside the embedded frame.

Use Open working preview. The embedded frame is deliberately isolated; the separate preview lets you test ordinary page links.

I want an earlier result.

Use Previous version or choose an item under Project versions. Accepted releases are saved with the project; making a new branch does not delete them.

I want to start over without losing made versions.

Use Clear draft. Abracadabra asks before replacing meaningful unsaved edits, while accepted releases remain saved with the project.
