Before you start

Bring the basics.

You need a business name, one clear sentence about what you do, one useful detail, and one way for customers to act.

What you do

Name + one plain sentence

Write for a customer who has never heard of the business.

Useful detail

Offer, area, hours, or About

Add what helps someone decide whether to contact you.

Next action

Phone, email, or website

Give visitors at least one clear way to continue.

  1. 01

    Basics

    Name and describe the business.

    Add the business name and one clear sentence explaining what it does.

  2. 02

    Details

    Add the facts that help someone decide.

    Add offers, service area, hours, or an About note. Leave out anything the page does not need.

  3. 03

    Contact

    Choose what visitors should do next.

    Add the phone, email, or website needed for the main action. Blank contact methods stay off the page.

  4. 04

    Look

    Choose the feel.

    Clear is clean and direct. Warm is friendly. Arcane is bold. The facts do not change when the look changes.

  5. 05

    Review

    Check the exact details.

    Read the summary before making the page. If you edit anything, Abracadabra asks you to review again.

  6. 06

    Preview

    Open the page and test its links.

    This is where Abracadabra ends. Continue to Alacazam only if you want to keep the page, shape it further, or put it online.

What the $5 buys

Abracadabra ends at the preview.

Five dollars makes the page and shows it to you. Alacazam is the separate paid service that customises it further, downloads it, or puts it online at an address you buy, bring, or rent — and the $5 is credited against it.

What can I do with the HTML file?

Keep a copy somewhere you control. You may modify it and host it yourself without another Site Sourcery payment. Domain, hosting, and made-for-you help are separate.

Quick help

If you get stuck.

Open only the answer you need.

Abracadabra did not open.

Reload the page. If it still does not open, use the Contact page and tell Zack which device and browser you used.

A field is highlighted.

Read the message above the form and correct that field. If you chose a main contact action, enter that contact detail too.

The review changed.

You edited a business detail. Read the new summary and approve it again.

A link does not work in the small preview.

Choose “Open working preview” to test the full page and its links.

I want an earlier version.

Use “Previous version” or choose one under Project versions.

I need more than one page.

Abracadabra makes one-page sites. Use the How a build runs for more pages, original layouts, or outside tools.
