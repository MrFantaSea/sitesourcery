Inside the maker

Four stages turn business details into a reviewed release.

The four-stage maker—Business, Look, Review, and Preview—happens before the account gate. Saving, choosing an address, publishing, management, and exit follow only after a visitor decides to keep the direction.

  1. 01

    Business

    Add the name, one useful sentence, supporting details, and at least one way for a visitor to act.

  2. 02

    Look

    Choose Clear, Warm, or Arcane. The presentation changes; the reviewed business details do not.

  3. 03

    Review

    Read the exact business details and chosen contact emphasis. A later change requires a fresh confirmation.

  4. 04

    Preview

    Make the page, revise it, restore a version, open the working result, and download the reviewed HTML.

Preview work and saved work are different. Before account creation, details and made versions stay in the open tab. When you choose to save, the reviewed preview can carry into the first local project; its draft and accepted releases then return in this browser until you delete them or the stated retention period ends.
