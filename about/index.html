How the work stays clear

How will the work stay clear?

Your call and email go to the person responsible for the quote and delivery. If a specialist, outside service, or customer-owned account is needed, the written scope says who controls it.

  1. 01
    Conversation

    Zack hears the situation and records the facts, desired result, limits, and questions.

  2. 02
    Quote

    The same person lists the size, design, tools, moving work, responsibilities, price, and exclusions.

  3. 03
    Making

    Planning, design, building, and reviews stay connected.

  4. 04
    Testing

    Pages, links, phone layouts, basic accessibility, claims, and agreed tools are checked.

  5. 05
    Handoff

    Files, access, ownership, use notes, exceptions, and any ongoing Care are made clear.
