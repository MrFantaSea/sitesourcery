Domains

Four ways to have an address.

Your website needs a name people can type. Buy one through Site Sourcery and run it yourself, buy one and have it looked after, bring a name you already own, or rent an address by the month. None of them locks you in.

Type a name and check whether the address is free. Checking costs nothing and reserves nothing.

    Compare the four ways

    The four ways

    Buy it, have it looked after, bring your own, or rent monthly.

    The first two are the same purchase. The only difference is who handles it afterward.

    Buy it from Zack

    Bought for you, run by you.

    Tell Zack the name you want. He checks what is available, tells you the exact price before anything is bought, buys it, and hands it over registered to you. From there it is yours to run, the same as if you had bought it direct.

    • One-time purchase
    • Price confirmed first
    • Registered in your name
    • You hold the keys

    Best when you want your own name and are comfortable handling the renewal yourself.

    Bought and looked after

    Bought for you, minded for you.

    Exactly the same purchase, except the renewals, the contact records, and the technical settings stay Zack's job instead of yours. Still registered in your name — being looked after is not the same as being owned.

    • Purchase plus upkeep
    • Renewals handled
    • Registered in your name
    • Stop any time

    Best when you never want to think about a renewal notice again.

    Bring your own

    Keep the name you have.

    Already own a domain? Leave it exactly where it is. It gets pointed at your new site, and it is checked to confirm it actually resolves before the job is called finished.

    • Nothing to transfer
    • Keep your registrar
    • Pointing handled
    • Checked before handoff

    Best when you already have a name, or someone already manages it for you.

    Rent an address

    your-name.sitesourcery.me

    An address on the Site Sourcery domain, billed monthly. Nothing to register, nothing to renew, and it is live immediately.

    • Live right away
    • Nothing to manage
    • Cancel any time
    • Upgrade later

    Best when you want to be online this week, or you are still testing whether a website earns its keep.

    Who owns it

    You are the owner on record.

    The plainest answer available, including the parts most people never tell you.

    • The domain is registered to you. Your name and your business go on the registration as the registrant — not Site Sourcery's. That holds whether you run it yourself or have it looked after.
    • You can leave. Ask, and you get the transfer authorization code and the domain unlocked so you can move it to any registrar you like. There is no fee to go and no foot-dragging.
    • Renewals are yearly, and you hear about them first. A domain you forget to renew is a domain you can lose, so it never arrives as a surprise.
    • Site Sourcery acts as your agent, not as the registrar. A domain bought on your behalf is placed through a Site Sourcery account at Spaceship. Spaceship or its provider is the ICANN-accredited registrar. Site Sourcery is not a registrar and claims no special access to the registry.
    • Your contact details go to the registrar. Registering a domain means giving a registrant name, address, email, and phone to the registrar, who is required to hold them. You are told exactly what gets sent, and you agree to it before it goes.
    • A rented address works differently. your-name.sitesourcery.me is a name on the Site Sourcery domain, so it stays Site Sourcery's — that is the trade for having nothing to manage. Move to a name of your own whenever you like and the site comes with you.

    Before anything is bought

    • Exact price, in writing
    • What data goes to the registrar
    • The registrar's terms to read
    • Your say-so, recorded

    Domain prices depend on the exact name and its ending, so the figure is confirmed for your name before anything is bought. Nothing is purchased on your behalf until you have seen that number and said yes to it.
