    Registration plus ongoing help

    Ask about a name with written upkeep.

    Registration and ongoing domain work are separate responsibilities. If both are available, the accepted scope states who controls the provider account, handles renewals and settings, receives notices, and approves changes.

    Best when you already have a name, or someone already manages it for you.

    Who owns it

    For a customer-owned domain, you remain the owner on record.

    The plainest answer available, including the parts most people never tell you.

    • The registrant is the customer. If Site Sourcery is authorized to register a customer-owned domain, your name or business goes on the registration as the registrant — not Site Sourcery's.
    • Transfer is governed by the provider. Eligibility, locks, authorization codes, timing, and any provider charges are checked and stated; a transfer outcome or deadline is never guaranteed.
    • Renewal terms must be explicit. The renewal cadence, exact renewal price, notices, and who is responsible are confirmed for the exact name and provider.
    • Site Sourcery is not the registrar. Any accepted registration scope names the actual provider and the authority Site Sourcery has to act. Site Sourcery claims no special access to a registry.
    • Registrant data goes only with authorization. Registration can require a name, address, email, and phone. You see what the provider requires and authorize its submission before it is sent.
    • A platform address works differently. your-name.sitesourcery.me remains a name on the Site Sourcery domain. Its availability and lifecycle come from the separately accepted service terms, not a domain purchase.
