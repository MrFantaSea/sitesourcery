    Who owns it

    For a customer-owned domain, you remain the owner on record.

    You should never have to wonder who owns your web address.

    • The registrant is the customer. If Site Sourcery is authorized to register a customer-owned domain, your name or business goes on the registration as the registrant — not Site Sourcery's.
    • The domain company handles transfers. Locks, transfer codes, timing, and fees depend on that company. I check those details before work starts.
    • You will know the renewal plan. I show the renewal price I can see, when it renews, and who is responsible for it.
    • Site Sourcery is not the registrar. I use a domain company to register the name and tell you which company it is.
    • You approve the information we send. Registration may need a name, address, email, and phone. I show you what is needed first.
    • A Site Sourcery address is different. your-name.sitesourcery.me is part of a Site Sourcery plan, not a domain you own.
