## Resulting behavior

Five event kinds cover a verified paid month, payment-problem changes, cancellation updates, request acknowledgment and request status. A paid notification is unique to an immutable provider-verified paid period; replay and concurrent reconciliation cannot create another for that period. Resolving a payment problem does not imply payment or create a paid receipt. The paid email says the payment is confirmed and directs the customer to the amount and dates in their account; it does not promise a downloadable receipt document.

The recipient comes from the agreement's bound customer. Durable source and outbox data contain IDs, state and digests, excluding recipient addresses, message bodies and private owner notes. A currently ineligible customer or project skips only notification creation so financial reconciliation still persists. Infrastructure or rendering errors remain transaction failures. Provider-scheduled cancellation does not invent a customer-requested state.
