Download and monthly plans

Download costs $20 once. Alakazam is $25, $35, or $50 a month and renews until you cancel.

Read full Download and monthly-plan terms

Abracadabra is free for making and viewing a private preview. A completed one-time $20 payment unlocks Download for one saved project. Later versions and repeat downloads from the same active project do not require another purchase. Download does not renew or start a subscription. An expired, declined, cancelled, or incomplete checkout creates no Download access.

The Download sale is final when the accepted HTML file is available through the signed-in Download control. Site Sourcery does not offer a refund for change of mind, nonuse, customer-supplied errors, or edits made after download. This does not limit a refund required by law or a remedy for a duplicate or unauthorized charge or a proven failure to provide the purchased file.

Alakazam offers $25, $35, and $50 monthly plans for one saved project. The checkout shows the chosen plan, tax, total, renewal, and included tools before payment. The plan renews each month at its current accepted price until the customer cancels. A prior $20 Download purchase creates one $20 credit toward the same project's first Alakazam bill. The credit has no cash value and cannot be moved to another project.

An upgrade charges the fixed difference between the current and new monthly tier when the customer approves it; the new full price applies at the next renewal. A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal, with no mid-month refund or credit. The customer keeps the already-paid higher tier through that date. Plan changes show the exact price and effective date before acceptance.

The customer may cancel Alakazam at any time with no cancellation fee. Cancellation stops the next renewal and takes effect at the end of the paid period. Service continues through that date. There is no ordinary partial-month refund or proration, except when required by law or for a duplicate or unauthorized charge or a proven service failure. A cancelled plan cannot be restored with an undo button; the customer may start a new plan separately.

If a renewal payment fails, Alakazam gives a seven-day payment grace period. During that time the customer can privately read and export saved work, but editing, publishing, and included Care pause. If payment is not fixed, the plan enters a 30-day exit window for private reading and export before eligible project content is removed. A downloaded HTML file remains under the customer's control.

A downloaded HTML file is under the customer’s control and must be moved or deleted through the customer’s browser, operating system, device, or chosen host.
