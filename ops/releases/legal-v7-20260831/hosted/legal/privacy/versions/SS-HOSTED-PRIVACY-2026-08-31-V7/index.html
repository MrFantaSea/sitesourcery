Domains and outside providers

The quick check uses Cloudflare DNS. If you hire us for a domain, the registrar receives the details needed to do the work.

Read full domain details

When you press the Domains page’s check button, the browser cleans the typed candidate and sends its .com, .net, and .org names in NS queries to Cloudflare’s public DNS-over-HTTPS resolver at cloudflare-dns.com. Cloudflare processes the query and connection data under its Public DNS Resolver privacy notice, including the resolver logging and limited APNIC access described there. A recursive resolver may contact authoritative DNS servers to answer.

The quick check does not call a registrar and does not prove availability, show a final price, reserve a name, or place an order. If a customer hires Site Sourcery to register, connect, renew, or transfer a domain, the chosen registrar or DNS provider may receive the approved domain, registrant and account contact details, billing information, verification responses, nameserver and DNS settings, renewal choices, transfer information, and support records needed for that work. Site Sourcery shares those details only after the customer approves the name, price, and job.

Read full retention details
