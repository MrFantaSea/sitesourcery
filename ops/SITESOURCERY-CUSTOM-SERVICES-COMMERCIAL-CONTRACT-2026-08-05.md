# Site Sourcery Custom and existing-site commercial contract — 2026-08-05

This is the canonical commercial contract for Custom builds and work on
websites that already exist. It turns the owner's August 5 rulings and the
reviewed recommendations into one implementation source of truth. Newer dated
owner rulings supersede this file. Historical catalogs and public copy do not.

This contract does not authorize a public offer, provider charge, deployment,
or production release by itself. A service becomes sellable only after its
account, quote, invoice/payment, job, customer, owner, failure, and recovery
journeys are implemented and tested.

## Rules shared by every paid service

- An anonymous visitor may ask a question. A paying client must create or use
  a Site Sourcery account before accepting a quote, receiving a payable
  invoice, paying, submitting private access material, or opening a support
  request. The account owns the durable client, site, quote, payment, job, and
  message history.
- No free assessment, diagnosis, takeover review, recovery investigation, or
  hidden work is promised. A brief inquiry may record what the customer says;
  Site Sourcery does not inspect or diagnose the site until paid scope exists.
- Every job has a written scope, exact customer, exact site or domain, included
  outcomes, exclusions, price, payment schedule, dependencies, and completion
  evidence before work begins.
- Customer domains, registrar accounts, mailbox accounts, Google Business
  Profiles, Search Console properties, and other provider accounts remain in
  the customer's name whenever the provider supports it. Site Sourcery uses
  delegated access where possible. Passwords, recovery codes, API keys, and
  other secrets are never requested through ordinary forms, email, or chat.
- Registrar, hosting, mailbox, software, advertising, tax, and other provider
  charges are separate from Site Sourcery labor unless the written quote says
  otherwise.
- The same work is charged once. Quote composition uses named overlap keys for
  site inventory, supportability review, backup baseline, DNS connection,
  redirect planning, search setup, and business-information intake.
- A starting price applies only inside its written boundary. Unknown,
  unsupported, unusually large, regulated, compromised, proprietary, or
  inaccessible systems receive a separately priced scope or a decline.
- No ranking, indexing, recovery, transfer date, uptime, emergency response,
  or compatibility outcome is guaranteed when a third party controls it.
- Site Sourcery does not advertise refunds. Agreements must describe what is
  earned at each delivered milestone, what remains uncharged if later work
  does not begin, how future renewals stop, and any rights required by
  applicable law. There is no blanket promise that overrides the law.

## Website assessment

### Standard assessment — $200 one time

The standard assessment covers:

- one customer, business, public website, and primary goal;
- up to five representative public pages or page types, not five arbitrary
  samples repeated across a very large site;
- desktop and phone review;
- up to ten important findings with screenshot evidence;
- severity, practical importance, and a recommended order of work; and
- one written report delivered on the date stated in the accepted offer.

There is no public standard delivery-time promise until the owner explicitly
freezes one. Each accepted offer must state its actual delivery date after
payment and required customer facts are ready.

It does not include repairs, an exhaustive page-by-page crawl, authenticated
admin or source-code review, malware cleanup, account recovery, penetration
testing, legal advice, or accessibility certification.

A larger, multi-location, application-like, ecommerce, membership, regulated,
or otherwise dense site receives a separately quoted expanded assessment
before the expanded inspection begins. The expanded-assessment pricing formula
is held for a later owner redline; no agent may invent one publicly.

### Build credit

- The full $200 may be applied once to one accepted Site Sourcery Custom base
  build from Card through Scale for the same customer, business, and site.
- The build must be accepted within 90 days of assessment delivery. After that
  date Site Sourcery may require and price a refresh before reissuing a credit.
- The credit has no cash value, is not transferable, cannot be applied twice,
  and does not pay provider costs, tax, ongoing service, or unrelated work.
- An assessment that also satisfies part of a later takeover/onboarding review
  prevents duplicate inspection charges. That is scope reuse, not a second
  cash credit.

## Website Rescue and Tune-Up

Rescue fixes selected findings on an existing site. It is not another name for
an assessment: the assessment identifies and prioritizes problems; Rescue is
the separately accepted repair work.

- A recent adequate Site Sourcery assessment is the normal prerequisite.
- A precisely known mechanical request may be quoted without a broad
  assessment only when the customer identifies the exact change and Site
  Sourcery does not need to diagnose its cause or surrounding risk.
- The customer may choose a safe subset of findings. Dependencies and fixes
  that would leave the site less safe must be disclosed before acceptance.
- Customer-facing quotes show fixed line items and outcomes, not unexplained
  internal units.

Internal pricing uses repair units:

| Internal class | Units | Price at $125 per unit |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Quick bounded correction | 1 | $125 |
| Ordinary technical repair | 2 | $250 |
| Complex repair | 4 or more | $500 or more |

The minimum Rescue scope is two units, or $250. A shared root cause is charged
once even when it repairs multiple symptoms. Work above eight units requires a
custom repair scope and a written repair-versus-rebuild comparison rather than
automatic unit stacking. Newly discovered scope requires a change order; it is
not silently performed or charged.

## Outside Website Management

Outside Website Management is for a site Site Sourcery did not build. It is a
separate product from Alakazam care and from optional Custom Care because the
technology, access, history, and takeover risk are different.

### Account and paid onboarding are mandatory

No outside site enters monthly management without a Site Sourcery account and
a completed paid onboarding. Onboarding is staged so unsupported work is not
sold and unperformed work is not charged:

1. The first $200 pays for a supportability and takeover review. It records the
   platform, ownership, access path, provider dependencies, current backup and
   recovery state, material risks, and whether Site Sourcery can responsibly
   accept the site.
2. If the site is declined, the client receives the written result and safer
   next options. No remaining onboarding balance or monthly plan is charged.
3. If the site is accepted, the remaining onboarding balance is charged before
   Site Sourcery establishes delegated access, a verified baseline backup where
   possible, monitoring, a maintenance baseline, and the first monthly record.

| Outside site class | Total onboarding | Monthly base |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Simple/static supported site | $300 | $125/month |
| Ordinary supported CMS | $600 | $225/month |
| Commerce, membership, custom application, or unusual stack | separately quoted, starting at $900 | separately quoted, starting at $400/month |

A still-current assessment may satisfy overlapping supportability facts, so
the same inspection is not billed twice. Critical Rescue work may be required
and quoted before management begins.

The monthly base is paid in advance and covers the responsibilities stated in
the plan: availability checks, backup/recovery checks where supported, routine
maintenance appropriate to the accepted platform, certificate/domain/provider
watching, one request queue, and a monthly work/health receipt. It does not
silently include content changes, redesign, new features, ordinary repair
labor, advertising, provider fees, or 24/7 emergency response.

Optional prepaid work capacity uses the same internal $125 repair unit:

- two units: add $250/month;
- four units: add $500/month; and
- larger or specialist capacity: custom quote.

Unused included capacity may roll into the immediately following monthly cycle
only, never indefinitely. Acknowledgement and completion timing must be stated
in the accepted plan or request; there is no public standard response-time
promise yet. Emergency or rush work is offered only when capacity is explicitly
confirmed.

Plan upgrades or downgrades take effect at the next renewal unless a written
change says otherwise. The client retains the current paid level through the
current period; no mid-period cash refund or proration is promised.

## Business Email and Domain work

This service configures, moves, or helps recover customer-owned domain and
email services. It does not duplicate the held domain storefront, which would
sell and renew names.

### Connect — $200

One customer-owned domain, one ordinary mailbox/provider destination, ordinary
DNS verification and mail-authentication records, up to three ordinary
mailboxes or aliases, and completion testing. It includes no historic-mail
migration, provider subscription, registrar purchase, or access recovery.

This reuses the existing `$200 additional connection` commercial component.
The old arbitrary five-DNS-record wording must be reconciled to this one-outcome
boundary rather than creating a duplicate SKU.

### Move — from $500

One domain, one source provider, one destination provider, up to three ordinary
mailboxes with up to 10 GB each, supported export/import, DNS cutover, send and
receive tests, and owner handoff. Each additional ordinary mailbox up to 10 GB
is $100. Larger archives, calendars, contacts, shared mailboxes/drives,
compliance retention, unsupported exports, and multi-domain moves are custom.

### Rescue — $300 investigation

The paid investigation maps registrar/provider ownership, current access,
verification evidence, recovery paths, and a written next-step plan. Actual
recovery, transfer, cleanup, or replacement is separately quoted. Successful
recovery and transfer timing are never guaranteed.

## Website Move or Platform Escape

This is a composed transition project, not a mystery all-inclusive SKU.

- The bounded website assessment is $200 when the existing site must be
  inventoried and risked.
- The transition base is $500 after assessment. It covers one source and one
  destination, working inventory, backup/export attempt, destination
  preparation, planned cutover, launch checks, a rollback plan where possible,
  and owner handoff.
- A rebuild uses the existing Card-through-Scale ladder. The assessment credit
  applies to that base build when eligible; it does not erase the transition
  labor.
- Existing redirect bands, data-migration bands, provider setup, connection,
  writing, and other real components are reused rather than renamed.
- Proprietary builders, ecommerce, memberships, logins, databases, automations,
  licensed themes/plugins, and missing source access require custom scope.

The inventory must name pages, content, accounts, redirects, DNS changes,
missing capabilities, launch checks, dependencies, and rollback limits. Site
Sourcery never promises that every source-platform feature can move.

## Local Presence

The concrete name is Local Presence, not a vague promise to "get found."

### Website Visibility Foundation — $400

One business, one location, one website, and up to five key pages. The written
scope may include a customer-owned Search Console property, sitemap,
robots/canonical/indexability checks, consistent public name/address/phone
facts, title and description cleanup, basic local-business structured data,
connection to the customer's Google Business Profile, a setup receipt, and one
later technical recheck.

### Google Business Profile setup or cleanup — $300

One ordinary eligible customer-owned profile, with the customer as owner and
Site Sourcery as manager where access is needed. This never includes fake
reviews, false locations, keyword stuffing, or a claim that Google charges the
Site Sourcery labor fee.

### Combined foundation — $650

The website and profile work above for one business/location/site. Additional
capacity is $150 per next five key pages and $250 per additional location,
subject to written scope.

For a new Site Sourcery Custom or Alakazam site, the baseline technical search
plumbing promised by that build must be implemented there and is not sold
again under a different name. Profile work, additional locations, larger-page
coverage, and work on an outside site remain separately chargeable. No search
engine ranking, indexing date, traffic, lead, or revenue result is guaranteed.

## Payment schedule

- Assessments, recovery investigations, and the first $200 outside-site
  supportability review are paid in full before that work begins.
- Card and Card Plus use their existing rule: after any valid assessment
  credit, the remaining balance is paid before build work begins.
- Site through Scale use 50% of the accepted project total before work and 50%
  before final launch or handoff; the assessment payment counts toward the
  first half when credited.
- Other one-time jobs below $1,000 are paid in full before work. One-time jobs
  of $1,000 or more use 50% before work and 50% before final launch, transfer,
  or handoff unless the written quote establishes safer milestones.
- Outside-site onboarding charges its $200 review first and only the remaining
  accepted onboarding balance afterward. Monthly management and prepaid work
  capacity are billed in advance.
- A changed scope requires a written change order and its required payment
  before the added work begins.

## One backend, not five disconnected backends

The owner Mac, owner Pixel, customer account, and later Desiderata Labs HQ all
use one durable commercial/work system. The minimum authority model is:

- client account, organization, membership, and customer-owned site asset;
- site origin: `alakazam`, `sitesourcery_custom`, or `external`;
- versioned service catalog with fixed, banded, custom, and recurring items;
- assessment, representative scope, findings, evidence, delivery, and
  expanded-assessment hold;
- outside-site supportability review and staged onboarding;
- selected findings and internally computed repair units;
- versioned estimate/quote, overlap keys, acceptance disclosure, and expiry;
- assessment-credit ledger and separate scope-satisfaction/deduplication
  records;
- invoice, deposit/milestone/balance, provider payment evidence, receipt, and
  reconciliation state;
- job, checklist, dependencies, access requests, change orders, deliverables,
  completion evidence, and handoff;
- recurring care/management agreement, renewal-bound plan change, included
  capacity, one-cycle rollover, usage, and cancellation state;
- tickets, monitoring checks, backup/restore evidence, incidents, and monthly
  receipts; and
- immutable owner/customer audit events for every commercial or operational
  authority change.

The browser supplies identifiers, choices, written acceptance, and idempotency
keys. It never supplies authoritative prices, credits, unit totals, invoice
status, payment success, entitlement, provider identity, or completion state.
Provider webhooks wake reconciliation; verified provider readback and one
atomic local transaction establish money authority.

## Required customer and owner journeys

- Customer: create account, identify the site, buy bounded assessment or paid
  onboarding review, receive findings/result, select work, review and accept
  exact quote, pay exact invoice, follow job status, answer safe access
  requests, approve change orders, receive completion/handoff, and view
  receipts.
- Outside-management customer: complete staged onboarding, resolve blockers,
  start a monthly plan, submit a request, see capacity use and rollover, review
  the monthly receipt, and schedule a plan change at renewal.
- Owner Mac/Pixel: find client/site, review evidence, classify findings, compose
  a deduplicated quote, issue invoice, see provider-confirmed payment, move the
  job through bounded states, request delegated access without collecting a
  password, record proof, create a change order, close/handoff work, manage
  monthly responsibility, and reconcile uncertainty without a casual
  mark-paid/grant-service button.

## Held decisions and release gates

- Expanded-assessment bands beyond the standard $200 boundary.
- Exact optional Custom Care plan names, prices, capacity, hosting boundary,
  and response promise after the included 30-day correction window.
- Exact Alakazam support/care quantities and response promises.
- Standard assessment delivery timing and Outside Website Management
  acknowledgement/response timing.
- Emergency/rush management response and any after-hours price.
- Final acceptance list for outside platforms beyond case-by-case review.
- Domain storefront registration/renewal economics and customer-domain use
  inside Alakazam.
- The Responder remains held until telephony/A2P fulfillment is real.

None of these held details may be guessed into public copy, provider products,
or customer invoices.
