How it works

Five simple steps.

The Responder handles the first reply. A person still handles the customer and the job.

  1. 01

    Call missed

    Your phone rings as usual. If nobody answers under the rule you chose, The Responder starts.

  2. 02

    Quick text

    The caller gets a text that names your business, says the call was missed, and lets them know a person will follow up.

  3. 03

    A few useful questions

    It can ask for a name, the kind of help needed, the service area, or how urgent the request is. You choose the questions.

    It does not make up prices, promise work, or pretend to be a person.

  4. 04

    Human handoff

    The reply and useful details go to the person you choose. Anything unusual waits for that person.

  5. 05

    Next step or stop

    The exchange ends when the next step is clear or the caller asks it to stop. The caller can opt out at any time.
