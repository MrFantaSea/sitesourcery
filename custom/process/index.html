Custom process

How a Custom website gets made.

For businesses considering a made-for-you website, this page explains each step from the first conversation through launch and handoff. Projects are inquiry-only. You receive the exact scope, price, timing, and payment terms in writing before work begins.

Ask Zack about a website

Six steps

  1. 01

    Understand the business

    Share your customers, services, location, current site, problems, and desired result.

  2. 02

    List the work

    Identify the needed pages, content, images, tools, and anything that must move from an old site.

  3. 03

    Set the direction

    Choose a restrained, distinctive, or fully art-directed look based on how the site should feel and move.

  4. 04

    Design the site

    Plan the page order, content, visual direction, phone layouts, and important visitor actions together.

  5. 05

    Build and refine

    Build the real pages, test them, and use each included review round to refine the agreed work.

  6. 06

    Launch and hand off

    Check pages, links, phone layouts, basic accessibility, tools, redirects, access, and agreed files before launch.

Before work begins

The quote must match the planned website.

The written scope lists the work so the design does not quietly promise more or less.

It covers pages, sections, layouts, design level, tools, moving work, responsibilities, timing, payment, and what is not included. Later requests that change the job are priced and approved before that added work begins.

Responsibilities

Each step says who does what.

Dates are confirmed after the scope is accepted and the needed payment, materials, access, and decision-maker are ready. Waiting for them can move the schedule.

See responsibilities at every step
01

First conversation

Collect the facts

You provide
Business facts, goals, limits, materials, and current-site details.
Studio provides
Questions, review, missing items, risks, and a first job outline.
Done when
Known facts, open questions, and assumptions are listed.
02

Scope and acceptance

Put the deal in writing

You provide
Corrections, priorities, required materials, and acceptance.
Studio provides
Website size, design, tools, moving work, exclusions, responsibilities, timing, and payment.
Done when
The quote is accepted, the required first payment is made—everything for Card and Card Plus, or half for Site and larger—and the needed inputs are ready.
03

Design approval

Choose the look

You provide
One grouped response from the decision-maker by the review date.
Studio provides
The agreed restrained, distinctive, or fully art-directed design samples.
Done when
The direction is approved or refined within an included round.
04

Build and review

Make the real pages

You provide
Approved words and images, needed access, and grouped feedback.
Studio provides
Phone-friendly pages, basic accessibility, content placement, and agreed connections.
Done when
Every quoted page and tool is finished or a new request is named.
05

Launch

Switch with care

You provide
Final approval and access for the customer-owned domain or outside service named in the quote.
Studio provides
Final checks, needed backups, redirects, launch steps, and a safe way back.
Done when
The approved site is at its agreed address or the old version is restored.
06

Handoff and Care

Leave ownership clear

You receive
Agreed files, an access list, redirect notes, use notes, and final checks.
Studio closes
Final payment, ownership transfer, remaining access, and listed exceptions.
Afterward
Ongoing edits, hosting, monitoring, or Care require written coverage.

Reviews and changes

Feedback improves the agreed work.

  • One list per round.The decision-maker sends one grouped list by the review date.
  • Included review.An included round improves the approved design, content placement, or build within the written limits.
  • New work.New pages, tools, content, design direction, or major restructuring get a written effect on price and timing first.
  • Schedule.Waiting for materials, access, approval, or payment can move the launch date.
