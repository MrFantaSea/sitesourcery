Choice 2 · Design level

How custom should it look and move?

The same website size can use a quiet design, a stronger custom look, or a more immersive visual approach.

Compare the three design levels

Essential

Clear and restrained

Custom layout, type, color, phone-friendly behavior, accessibility, performance, and simple useful interactions. No original art direction or motion system is included.

Distinctive

A stronger original look

One original design direction, custom main sections, supporting graphics, and up to three useful motion pieces.

Immersive and art-directed

Several major compositions, a custom graphic system, and up to eight motion pieces with a reduced-motion option. Video, complex 3D, games, and app screens are separate work.

Choice 3 · Forms and tools

What must the site do?

Forms, booking, catalogs, payment tools, email, measurement, and custom controls add work beyond the page count.

See common items that may change the quote
Basic contact form

One recipient and up to five fields. Uploads, branching, accounts, and added automation are not included.

Small custom tool

One clearly defined tool with agreed information going in and a clear result coming out. Forms and outside services are counted separately.

Outside service setup

One client-owned account, one location or resource, a limited catalog, one visitor path, and a completed test.

Repeated item list

One supplied group of items in one repeated layout. Editing tools, filters, and separate item pages are not included.

Additional writing help

Light writing and editing from facts you supply and approve. Research, interviews, regulated claims, and major new writing are separate.

Additional connection

One ordinary customer-owned domain or service connection, including needed records, checks, redirects, and launch review.

Additional review round

One grouped round within the approved direction. New pages, tools, content, or a new direction change the scope.

Rush scheduling, if available

This can be offered only after Zack confirms time is available and the complete materials, accepted quote, and required payment are ready.

Choice 4 · Replacing an old site

What must move or stay working?

A replacement may affect old links, words, images, data, domains, accounts, and familiar customer paths. The quote names what will move and who handles it.

Your web address stays in your business’s name.

If domain work is included, the quote says who handles the provider account, renewal, access, and a move. Site Sourcery does not become the owner.
