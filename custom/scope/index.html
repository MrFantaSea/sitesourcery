Axis one · Footprint

What must exist?

We inventory the real public surface: source pages, delivered pages, sections, distinct layouts, words, media, forms, locations, collections, and legal or utility needs. Each existing route receives a keep, merge, redirect, or retire decision.

These are delivered-footprint ceilings, not stacked packages.

A dense page or unusual layout can require a larger footprint even when the page count is smaller. The written quote prices the chosen footprint together with creative direction, components, and transition work.

Axis two · Creative direction

How expressive should it feel?

Creative direction is chosen independently from site size. The same footprint can be composed with quiet precision, a stronger custom identity, or immersive art direction.

Essential

Clear, restrained, exact

A polished custom layout system, typography, color, responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, and restrained functional interactions. No bespoke art direction or motion system is implied.

Distinctive

Memorable by design

One original art direction, custom hero and compositions, supporting graphics, and up to three purposeful motion components in the approved footprint.

Atelier

Immersive and art-directed

Several major compositions, a custom graphic system, and up to eight ambient or scroll-led motion components with reduced-motion treatment. Video, complex 3D, games, and application interfaces remain separate scope.

Axis three · Systems and data

What must the site connect or operate?

Forms, booking, catalogs, commerce, email routes, structured data, analytics, collections, automation boundaries, and bespoke interfaces each create different work and risk. Nothing is implied by a page count.

Basic contact form

One recipient and up to five fields, without uploads, branching, accounts, or hidden automation.

Standard interactive tool

One documented input/output model within a bounded implementation; ordinary forms and embeds stay separate.

Hosted-provider setup

One client-owned account, one location or resource, a bounded catalog, one journey, and test completion.

Static collection

One supplied collection in one reusable layout, without CMS publishing, filtering, or separate detail routes.

Additional content shaping

Light drafting and editing from supplied, approved facts. Research, interviews, regulated claims, and substantial new writing remain separate.

Additional connection

One ordinary client-owned domain or provider endpoint with bounded records, verification, redirect, and release checks.

Additional revision round

One grouped refinement round within the approved direction. New pages, capabilities, content, or direction are a scope change.

Priority production window

Available only after capacity, complete materials, acceptance, and the required starting payment are confirmed.

Axis four · Transition and migration

How does the business cross over safely?

A replacement site affects old URLs, content, media, data, domain records, accounts, and established customer paths. Redirects, migration, cutover sequencing, and ownership handoffs are named rather than buried.

  1. 01

    Map the old surface

    Record the routes, content, collections, tools, accounts, and dependencies that already exist.

  2. 02

    Define the destination

    Match every kept fact and required behavior to its place in the delivered site.

  3. 03

    Plan the crossing

    Specify redirects, migration responsibilities, domain or account changes, verification, and rollback boundaries.
