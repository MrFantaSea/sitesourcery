How scope works

What changes the quote?

The number of pages is only one part of the work.

  • Size: how much of the website must be made.
  • Design: how custom and expressive it should feel.
  • Tools: forms, outside services, and other agreed features.
  • Moving work: content, links, domains, and launch needs.
The deal is clear before work starts.

Card and Card Plus, the one-page sizes, are paid in full before work starts. Site through Scale use half before work starts and half on completion. Dates are confirmed after the needed payment, materials, access, and decision-maker are ready. The agreed client deliverables become yours after final payment; the quote lists any exceptions. A customer-owned web address stays in the customer’s name.

What it costs

Where a build starts.

A starting figure for each size. The written quote is the number that counts, and it comes before any work.

By how much site there is

  • Assessment — $200. Not a build. A written report on the site you already have, and the full $200 comes off whichever rung you climb to next. Book one.
  • Card — $400. One page that says who you are and how to reach you.
  • Card Plus — $650. One page with more to say, and room to say it.
  • Site — $1,200. A handful of pages that work as a set.
  • Site Plus — $1,800. More pages, more structure, more ways in.
  • Signature — $2,800. Original art direction throughout.
  • Flagship — $4,000. The full build, top to bottom.

Two things move the price beyond size, and both are stated in the quote rather than discovered later:

  • How far the art direction goes. The standard level is included. Distinctive adds 25%, Atelier adds 50% — each with a minimum, because original design work has a floor below which it cannot be done properly.
  • Moving off a site you already have. Carrying over content, links, and an address without losing what the old site earns you is real work, and it is priced as its own line.

Card and Card Plus are paid in full before work starts. Site and above are half before, half on completion.
