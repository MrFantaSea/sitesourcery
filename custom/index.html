The honest shape of the job

Size and spectacle are different decisions.

A broad, restrained site can require more work than a tiny immersive one. A small site can also demand intense illustration, animation, or interaction.

That is why footprint, creative direction, systems and data, and transition or migration remain separate. Card through Scale each have exact delivered-footprint ceilings; Essential, Distinctive, and Atelier each have exact creative boundaries. The written quote reconciles those choices instead of pretending one package name explains the whole job.
