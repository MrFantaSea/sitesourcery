How pricing works

What changes the quote?

The number of pages is only one part of the work.

A one-page site can still need original design. A larger site can still use a simple look.

  • Size: how much of the website must be made.
  • Design: how custom and expressive it should feel.
  • Tools: forms, outside services, and other agreed features.
  • Moving an existing site: content, links, domains, and launch needs.
The deal is clear before work starts.

One-page builds are paid in full before work starts. Larger builds use half before work and half after completion, before the final handoff. Completion does not trigger an automatic charge.

Dates are confirmed after payment, materials, access, and the decision-maker are ready. The finished work becomes yours after final payment. A domain you own stays in your name.

Website sizes

  • Assessment — $350. Not a build. You get written findings, screenshots, and a list of what matters most. The price covers one agreed website review. If you hire Site Sourcery for an eligible build within 90 days, the full $350 goes toward it. Ask about an assessment.
  • Card — $350. One page that says who you are and how to reach you.
  • Card Plus — $600. One page with more sections and content.
  • Site — $1,000. A small website with several pages.
  • Site Plus — $1,600. More pages for services, products, and business information.
  • Signature — $2,400. Deeper content across more pages.
  • Flagship — $3,600. A larger website with more pages and content.

Design level and moving an existing site can add to the price. Both appear in your quote:

  • Design level. The standard level is included. Distinctive adds 25% and Atelier adds 50%. Minimum charges also apply and are listed in your quote.
  • Moving an existing site. Moving content, preserving links, and connecting your domain are priced separately.

Card and Card Plus are paid in full before work starts. Site through Scale use half before work starts; the final half becomes due only after completion and before final handoff. Completion itself does not authorize an automatic charge.
