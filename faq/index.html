What is the difference between Custom, Abracadabra, and Hive?

Custom is a website made for you through a tailored studio engagement. The current Abracadabra route is a device-local working rehearsal: it makes and exports real HTML and rehearses account, release, rollback, and exit state, but it does not host or publicly publish. A hosted account and public provider publication belong only to a separately released service. Hive is for working systems—the public planner lets you inspect six bounded workflow blueprints without activating an integration.

Can I use Abracadabra right now?

The private local rehearsal can be used now on this device. Create a local account and project, enter and review the facts, make real self-contained HTML, save accepted releases in this browser, rehearse publication and rollback state, open a local preview, and export the project or HTML. This build does not represent a hosted public service as live: it creates no outside publication, hosted account, real charge, subscription, payment receipt, outside email, DNS change, or provider action. Multi-tab writing is unsupported and not prevented, so use one owner tab; the local store is non-transactional, non-durable, and non-authoritative.

What are Abracadabra’s two address choices?

For a separately released hosted service, Mode A would license use of an available label.sitesourcery.me address and Mode B would use a domain the customer owns—bought through Site Sourcery with the customer named as registrant or connected from another registrar. The current private rehearsal only saves a registrar-receipt or DNS-challenge handoff on this device. It does not contact a reviewer, configure an address, change DNS, or create a provider record. A customer-owned domain remains the customer’s.

Can the Abracadabra site be private?

The current device-local rehearsal can mark a release access-controlled and open its accepted HTML through the local viewer after a project passphrase. It does not create a private or public Internet address. In a separately released hosted service, an access-controlled release would require the project passphrase; it still would not be a vault for secrets, payment-card data, health information, or sensitive customer records.

What happens after a missed Abracadabra payment?

The current build rehearses the lifecycle locally and takes no payment. Under a separately released hosted-service agreement, an eligible published site would keep serving for 14 days, suspension would begin on day 15, and the project and full export would remain retained for 90 days after suspension before saved draft and release content are deleted. Cancellation would start that same retained exit period, with export, customer-domain detachment, and earlier deletion available where they apply.

How is Custom work scoped?

First choose the closest footprint: Card, Card Plus, Site, Site Plus, Signature, Flagship, or Scale. Then shape the creative direction—Essential, Distinctive, or Atelier—plus systems and data, and transition or migration. Those axes become one written quote instead of hiding the job inside page count.

How do Custom payments and ownership work?

Card and Card Plus are paid in full before work starts. Site, Site Plus, Signature, Flagship, and Scale use 50% before work starts and 50% on completion. Ownership of the agreed client deliverables transfers after final payment, subject to third-party materials, licenses, and any retained Site Sourcery tools identified in the agreement.

How long does Custom take?

The production window is stated in the written quote before acceptance and depends on the selected footprint, creative direction, systems, migration, supplied materials, and current studio capacity. Work begins when the accepted scope, required materials, and starting payment are in place; Site Sourcery does not give every size of project the same blanket deadline.

Who owns the client accounts, domain, and business data?

The client keeps its business data and client-owned outside accounts. A customer-owned domain uses the customer as registrant. Custom deliverable ownership transfers after final payment under the written agreement. Site Sourcery keeps its own name, product code, reusable systems, and other retained tools identified in the agreement.

What is the website assessment?

The $350 website assessment applies Site Sourcery’s real audit method to an existing public site: written findings, screenshot evidence, and severity ranking. Exact site scope and turnaround are stated before purchase. The full amount is credited toward any later accepted Site Sourcery build. It diagnoses; fixes require a separately accepted build or service scope.

Is ongoing Care automatically part of a website build?

No. Maintenance, monitoring, content changes, backups, and response responsibilities require their own written scope. A build does not silently create an ongoing Care commitment.

Does the Hive planner run my business automation?

No. It is a planning instrument. It displays a problem, trigger, allowed actions, consent concern, hard boundary, human handoff, and kill switch for each cell. No message, booking, review request, invoice action, provider connection, or other live effect is activated.

What should I send first?

A short description of the business, what is not working, what must be true when the work is complete, and any important date or existing system is enough to begin. Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive customer data.
