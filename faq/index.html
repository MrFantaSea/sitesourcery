What is the difference between Custom, Abracadabra, and Hive?

Custom is a quoted website project made for you. Abracadabra works only in this browser: it makes a real page you can download, but it does not put the page online or take payment. Hive shows six plans for common business handoffs; it does not turn on a live system.

Can I use Abracadabra right now?

Yes. Abracadabra currently works only in this browser. You can enter facts, choose a look, review the page, and download the result. It does not create an online account, publish a site, take payment, send email, or change a domain.

Can Abracadabra connect an online address?

No. The current version does not create or connect an Internet address and does not change domain settings. A customer-owned domain stays the customer’s. Any hosted address service would require a separate release and agreement.

Can the Abracadabra page be private?

No online site is created. The preview stays in this browser, and the downloaded HTML is an ordinary file. Do not use either one to hold passwords, card details, health information, or sensitive customer records.

What happens after a missed Abracadabra payment?

Nothing in the current version because it takes no payment and has no subscription. Any later hosted service would require a separate agreement with its billing, suspension, export, and deletion rules stated before purchase. See the website terms for the full legal detail.

How is Custom work scoped?

The quote combines four choices: website size, design level, forms and tools, and anything that must move from an old site. See the Custom scope page for exact limits.

How do Custom payments and ownership work?

Card and Card Plus are paid in full before work starts. Site, Site Plus, Signature, Flagship, and Scale use 50% before work starts and 50% on completion. Ownership of the agreed client deliverables transfers after final payment, subject to third-party materials, licenses, and any retained Site Sourcery tools identified in the agreement.

How long does Custom take?

Your written quote states the schedule before you accept it. Timing depends on the website size, design, tools, moving work, materials, and studio capacity. Work begins after the scope is accepted and the required materials and starting payment are ready.

Who owns the accounts, domain, and business data?

You keep your business data and customer-owned accounts. A customer-owned domain names the customer as registrant. Agreed Custom deliverables transfer after final payment. The agreement identifies any third-party licenses and Site Sourcery tools that are not transferred.

What is the website assessment?

The $350 website assessment reviews an existing public site. You receive written findings, screenshots, and an order of importance. Exact site scope and turnaround are stated before purchase. The full amount is credited toward a later accepted Site Sourcery build. Fixes need a separate written scope.

Is ongoing Care part of a website build?

No. Maintenance, monitoring, content changes, backups, and response duties need their own written scope. A website build does not automatically include ongoing Care.

Does the Hive planner run a live business system?

No. It shows the problem, starting event, allowed actions, limits, human handoff, and stop control for each plan. It does not send a message, book an appointment, request a review, change an invoice, or connect an outside service.

What should I send first?

Send the business name, what is not working, the result you want, any important date, and links to public materials that already exist. Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive customer data.
