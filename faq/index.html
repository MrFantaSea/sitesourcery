How much does a Custom website cost?

Custom has no one public set price. Your written quote combines four choices: website size, design level, forms and tools, and anything that must move from an old site. You see that price before you agree. See the Custom scope page for the exact limits that shape a quote.

How do Custom payments and ownership work?

Card and Card Plus are paid in full before work starts. Site through Scale use 50% before work; the final 50% becomes due after completion and before final handoff. It is not charged merely because completion was recorded. Ownership of the agreed client deliverables transfers after final payment, subject to third-party materials, licenses, and retained Site Sourcery tools identified in the agreement.

How long does Custom take?

Your written quote states the schedule before you accept it. Timing depends on the website size, design, tools, moving work, materials, and studio capacity. Work begins after the scope is accepted and the required materials and starting payment are ready.

Who owns the accounts, domain, and business data?

You keep your business data and customer-owned accounts. A customer-owned domain names the customer as registrant. Agreed Custom deliverables transfer after final payment. The agreement identifies any third-party licenses and Site Sourcery tools that are not transferred.

What is the website assessment?

The $350 website assessment delivers written findings, screenshot evidence, and an order of importance for the existing public site. The exact site scope and turnaround are stated before sale. Fixes need a separate accepted build or service scope. The full non-cash $350 may be credited once toward an accepted Site Sourcery build for the same customer, business, and site under the written credit rules.

Is ongoing upkeep part of a website build?

A Custom build includes a 30-day workmanship correction window. It begins only at final handoff, after the final balance is paid; completion or launch by itself does not start the clock. Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, content changes, backups, and response duties are separate. Optional Custom Care details and prices remain held.

Will the $5 download include a domain or hosting?

No. The planned $5 download would cover the HTML file only. Alakazam’s approved service address is a label.sitesourcery.me hostname, but subscriptions remain held. Is the Abracadabra preview private?

The free preview stays in the editor tab. Downloading creates an ordinary HTML file but does not put it on the Internet. Do not use the editor or file to hold passwords, card details, health information, or sensitive customer records.
