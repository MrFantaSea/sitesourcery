Hosted recurring service, cancellation, and nonpayment

The current tool takes no payment. Any hosted plan must show price and renewal terms before a charge.

Read full billing and cancellation terms

The current owner-side activation control changes only local rehearsal state and creates no provider reference, subscription, payment receipt, or charge. If a hosted recurring service is separately released, its selected plan, amount, interval, renewal behavior, and cancellation method must be shown before a real charge is authorized. Under those separately accepted terms, cancellation would stop later renewals and begin one 90-day retained exit period; repeating cancellation would not restart that clock. An eligible site would keep serving for 14 days after a missed payment, suspension would begin on day 15, and project content and export would remain retained for 90 days after suspension before deletion. A customer-owned domain would remain the customer’s and could be detached during retained exit. None of those provider-side serving, billing, restoration, or deletion effects occurs in this build.

Project deletion is terminal in the current device-local tool. It clears that project’s name and content, drafts, saved versions and release bytes, screening and publication records, access credential, domain-proof references, safety narratives, billing-restoration reference, and project support notes; it keeps only minimal identifiers, accepted-term and lifecycle timestamps, a text-free operator and safety timeline, and removal counts. A licensed Site Sourcery label and hostname are released for reuse. A customer-owned domain name may remain as an ownership fact, detached from serving and without its saved proof references. Deleted projects cannot be changed, published, restored, appealed, or served. Deleting one project does not delete the separate browser-local account, another project, or records outside this browser.
