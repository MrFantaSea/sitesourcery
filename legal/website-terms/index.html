Address modes

There are four ways to have an address. A domain bought for you is registered in your name, not Site Sourcery’s.

Read full hosted-address terms

Alacazam offers four ways to have an address. Rented: a licensed label.sitesourcery.me address, usable while the service stays active; the customer does not own that label or the sitesourcery.me domain. Bought and self-managed: Site Sourcery buys a domain on the customer’s instruction and the customer is named registrant. Bought and looked after: the same purchase, with renewals and technical records handled by Site Sourcery. Supplied: a domain the customer already holds, connected without transfer.

For any domain bought on a customer’s behalf, Site Sourcery acts as the customer’s authorised agent and not as a registrar. Spaceship, or its underlying provider, is the ICANN-accredited registrar; Site Sourcery claims no accreditation and no privileged registry access. Registration requires giving registrant name, postal address, email, and telephone to that registrar, which is obliged to hold them; the customer is told what will be sent and consents before it is sent. Availability is never reserved by a search, and is confirmed again immediately before purchase.

Hosted recurring service, cancellation, and nonpayment

Abracadabra is $5 once. Alacazam is $25 a month and can be cancelled at any time. Price and renewal are shown before any charge.

Read full billing and cancellation terms

Abracadabra is free to use for making and viewing a preview. A single payment of $5 unlocks downloading that preview. It is not a subscription, is not charged per version or per click, and does not renew. If the payment is not completed, nothing downloads and nothing is charged. Alacazam is $25 per month for keeping a page online. The amount, interval, renewal behaviour, and cancellation method are shown before any charge is authorised. Where a preview payment precedes an Alacazam subscription, the $5 is credited against the first Alacazam payment.

A domain bought on the customer’s behalf is priced per name and quoted in writing before purchase, because registry pricing varies by name and ending; it is never billed at an assumed figure. A website assessment is $200 and the full amount is credited against a later accepted build. Custom builds are quoted in writing before work begins: the two one-page sizes are payable in full before work starts, and larger sizes are half before and half on completion.

Cancelling Alacazam stops later renewals and begins a single 90-day retained exit period; cancelling again does not restart that clock. After a missed payment a site keeps serving for 14 days, suspension begins on day 15, and project content and export stay retained for 90 days after suspension before deletion. A customer-owned domain remains the customer’s throughout and can be pointed elsewhere at any time, including during retained exit.

Before an Alacazam subscription exists there is no saved project: refreshing or closing the tab clears the temporary facts and made versions. A downloaded HTML file is under the customer’s own control and must be moved or deleted through their browser, operating system, or device.
