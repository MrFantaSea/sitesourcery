Tenures, quotes, payment, cancellation, and nonpayment

Rent, Own, and Owned + managed are different choices; the server shows exact current amounts and renewal terms before payment.

Read billing and cancellation terms

Rent is recurring. It licenses the Spark site and includes eligible platform hosting while current. Own is a one-time purchase of the agreed Spark deliverable after final payment; it does not include platform hosting, a domain, or later changes. Owned + managed combines a one-time ownership amount with recurring eligible hosting and management. Cancelling its recurring part does not undo ownership already transferred after the final one-time payment.

There is no free trial. An expiring server quote identifies the exact website line items, one-time and recurring totals, interval, renewal behavior, included service, address eligibility, accepted document versions, and cancellation method. A domain keeps its own price, terms, consent, and receipt even when paid in the same customer checkout. Secure payment is available only when the approved offer, processor, and server checkout are enabled. A disabled checkout creates no charge.

Cancellation stops later renewals and starts one 90-day retained exit period; repeating cancellation does not restart it. An eligible recurring site continues serving for 14 days after a missed payment and is suspended on day 15 if payment remains unresolved. Paying an eligible balance during retention can restore service after the server rechecks entitlement and address state. Project content and export remain retained for 90 days after suspension before deletion. A customer-owned domain remains the customer’s and can be detached during exit.
