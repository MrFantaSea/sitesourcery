Rent

Keep it hosted by Site Sourcery.

Use recurring hosting for one Spark page with an eligible Site Sourcery hostname or your own domain. Cancel from your account.

Own

Buy the website files.

Pay once and download the finished site. Hosting, a domain, and ongoing changes are separate.

Owned + managed

Own it and let Site Sourcery run it.

You own the website files. If you use your own domain, the domain stays yours. Hosting and care continue under the plan you approve.

The exact price and what is included appear before payment. Checkout remains unavailable until a server-approved Spark offer and payment rail are enabled.
