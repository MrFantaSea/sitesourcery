Before you start

Bring the basics.

You need a business name, one clear sentence about what you do, one useful detail, and one way for customers to act.

What you do

Name + one plain sentence

Write for a customer who has never heard of the business.

Useful detail

Offer, area, hours, or About

Add what helps someone decide whether to contact you.

Next action

Phone, email, or website

Give visitors at least one clear way to continue.

  1. 01

    Business

    Say what customers need to know.

    Add the name, short description, offers, service area, hours, and contact details. Blank fields stay off the page.

  2. 02

    Look

    Choose the feel.

    Clear is clean and direct. Warm is friendly. Arcane is bold. The facts do not change when the look changes.

Quick help

If you get stuck.

Open only the answer you need.

Abracadabra did not open.

Reload the page. If it still does not open, use the Contact page and tell Zack which device and browser you used.

A field is highlighted.

Read the message above the form and correct that field. If you chose a main contact action, enter that contact detail too.

The review changed.

You edited a business detail. Read the new summary and approve it again.

A link does not work in the small preview.

Choose “Open working preview” to test the full page and its links.

I want an earlier version.

Use “Previous version” or choose one under Project versions.

I need more than one page.

Abracadabra makes one-page sites. Use Custom for more pages, original layouts, or integrations.
